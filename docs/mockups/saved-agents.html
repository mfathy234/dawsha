Removed from Dawsha with its run history. The file in ~/.claude/agents stays on disk.
What changes
  • The Orchestrator is removed: its boards, runs, history, settings and MCP server. The Lead replaces it: one agent you give a goal to, which hands parts to crew members only when it needs them.
  • Agents stays as the live view of background sub-agents. Crew is the people you keep.
Built in
  • Eight ready members, each with a sensible model, permissions and instructions you can copy and change: Maya (Lead), Sami (code reviewer), Tarek (debugger), Omar (builder), Hana (tester), Lina (UI polisher), Nour (docs and releases) and Yara (explainer). Built-ins can be hidden but not deleted. Duplicate one to change it.
The Lead
  • You give Maya a goal and a project. She writes a short plan saying who does what, on which model and at what estimated cost, and waits for you to approve it (you can switch that off).
  • Your defaults are built in: a few big jobs instead of many small ones, one review at the end, and at most two members working at once.
  • She runs the members as normal panes through the dawsha crew command, so each job shows in the grid, Agents, the timeline and usage.
  • She asks you before pushing, merging, posting to Azure DevOps, or going over the budget, and only you can answer.
  • "If needed": every member can still be run directly without the Lead.
Live state
  • Working: the ring breathes. Needs you: three flashes, then steady. Done: one flash, then ✓. Failed: ✕. Each always comes with a word, and the flashes stop with reduced motion.
